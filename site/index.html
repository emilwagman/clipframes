Three ways to point at something.

⌃⇧1

Element

Click one thing, like a button or a card. Clipframes saves a screenshot and reads the element's name and role. In browsers and Electron apps it also reads the DOM id, the classes and the page URL.

⌃⇧2

Screenshot

Drag an area. Clipframes saves the image and lists the elements inside it, so the agent knows what it is looking at.

⌃⇧3

Clip

Record an area and press the shortcut again to stop. Clipframes saves the video, a set of frames and a timeline of what you clicked.

Your captures stay on your Mac.

Each capture is a folder in ~/Clipframes with the images and a notes.md file that your agent reads. Nothing is uploaded.

The app goes online only to check for updates, and you can turn that off in Settings. The source is on GitHub.

Try it on your own app.
