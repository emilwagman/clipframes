Clipframes UX lab

Every page runs the real interface over a demo page in the browser. Ctrl+Shift+Space stands in for the global shortcut; the black chip at the top of each page does the same. Quit the Clipframes app first, or its own shortcut will answer.

The whole flow, three ways

  • TodayThe bar, a pick, a comment box. Unchanged code from desktop/ui.
  • Alternative A: write and pointOne box to write in. Click things as you write and they land in the sentence. Drag for a screenshot, type @ to find by name, Enter copies.
  • Alternative B: the trayNo rounds. Hold Option (Alt on Windows) and click things; they collect in a tray in the corner. Write the comments there, then copy.
