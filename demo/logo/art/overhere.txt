Logo mark design in the tradition of Paul Rand and the original 1984 Macintosh: one bold, confident, flat shape with a single clever idea, drawn by a master logo designer. Perfect solid black on a plain warm off-white paper background, centred, filling about 40 percent of the frame with generous empty space. No readable text, no letters except where described, no extra marks, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no gradient, no 3D, no mockup. The mark: a bold sign-painter's arrow, thick and confident, that rises and then bends sharply down to point at one spot, like a hand-painted sign saying over here. Flat black, crisp edges, a little playful.
