Logo mark design in the tradition of Paul Rand and the original 1984 Macintosh: one bold, confident, flat shape with a single clever idea, drawn by a master logo designer. Perfect solid black on a plain warm off-white paper background, centred, filling about 40 percent of the frame with generous empty space. No readable text, no letters except where described, no extra marks, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no gradient, no 3D, no mockup. The mark: a lowercase letter v drawn as two thick strokes that meet at a sharp point, and directly below the point, a single small solid circle, as if the v is a gesture pointing down at one exact spot: here it is. Elegant proportions, slightly calligraphic weight contrast between the two strokes.
