Logo concept presentation for a Mac app called Clipframes, in the spirit of the great single-silhouette marks of the 1970s and 80s: one simple shape with one witty idea. A solid black square with gently rounded corners, and cut out of it as negative space, a classic mouse pointer arrow, so the paper colour shows through in the shape of the cursor. The arrow sits slightly off-centre toward the lower right, tilted at the usual cursor angle, crisp and well proportioned. Perfectly flat, only black and a plain warm off-white paper background. The mark fills about 45 percent of the frame with generous empty space around it. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no gradient, no 3D, no mockup.
