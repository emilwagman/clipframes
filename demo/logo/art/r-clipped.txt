Logo concept presentation for a Mac app called Clipframes, in the spirit of Paul Rand's corporate marks: one bold, confident geometric emblem with a single clever idea. A thick solid black square frame (like a picture frame or a selected element, with a square hole in the middle), where one corner has been cleanly snipped off at a 45 degree angle, as if clipped with scissors; the small cut-off triangle sits just beside the corner, slightly lifted away. Perfectly flat, precise geometry, perfect black on a plain warm off-white paper background. The mark fills about 45 percent of the frame with generous empty space around it. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no gradient, no 3D, no mockup.
