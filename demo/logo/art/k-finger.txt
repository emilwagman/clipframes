Logo concept presentation for a Mac app called Clipframes, in the spirit of 1984 Macintosh icon design: crafted, witty, warm, made by a human icon artist. A single black mark, centred, on a plain warm off-white paper background: a small hand with the index finger extended, drawn as crisp black pixel art on a visible 32 by 32 grid, its fingertip just touching the edge of a small black-outlined rectangle (a user-interface element), as if pointing it out. Charming proportions, a soft knuckle line, a tiny cuff at the wrist. Original drawing, not a copy of any existing cursor. Only black and the paper colour. The mark fills about 45 percent of the frame with generous empty space around it. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no gradient, no mockup device.
