Hand-lettered logo for a friendly Mac app that lets you point at things on your screen. The lowercase word "clipframes" written in a casual, confident handwritten print with a marker pen, and a single loose hand-drawn oval loop circling the whole word, as if someone circled it on a screenshot to point it out; the loop overlaps itself where it closes. Spelled exactly c-l-i-p-f-r-a-m-e-s. Black ink only on a plain white background, centred, generous empty space. No other text, no arrows, no watermark, no border, no shadow, no texture, no mockup.
